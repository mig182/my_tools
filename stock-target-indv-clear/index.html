Change = (Sell Price - Buy Price) × Quantity.
Change % = (Sell Price - Buy Price) / Buy Price × 100.
Change represents gross profit or loss before fees and taxes.

Note: Quantity can be fractional. All prices must be nonnegative, quantity must be positive, and Change % cannot be below -100%.
