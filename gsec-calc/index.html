Indian G-Sec Calculator

Calculate Government Securities returns, discounts, T-Bill yields and annualised returns for custom days.

Calculations update automatically. All amounts are in Indian Rupees (₹).

Calculation Results

Calculation Formula

Holding Period Profit / Loss

Sale Price + Coupon Income − Purchase Price

Holding Period Return (%)

(Profit / Purchase Price) × 100

Annualised Return (%)

[(1 + Holding Period Return) ^ (365 / Days) − 1] × 100

Discount Amount

Face Value − Purchase Price

T-Bill Investment Yield (%)

[(Face Value / Purchase Price) − 1] × (365 / Days) × 100

T-Bill Bank Discount Yield (%)

(Discount / Face Value) × (365 / Days) × 100

Annualised investment yield uses compound annualisation. T-Bill investment yield and bank discount yield are different conventions.
